About Deep Proof

Make the reasoning part of the decision.

Deep Proof is a document-review platform for teams making high-stakes, evidence-led decisions: investment committees, credit teams, auditors, procurement boards and investigators.

It combines an open-source foundation with tailored, validated AI Review Skills for specific review workflows. An AI Review Skill brings together review instructions, analysis tools, verification cases and the calibration needed for a particular organisation and project type.

What we believe

  • Reasoning should be inspectable. A finding without its evidence is an opinion. Every claim Deep Proof makes points back to its source.
  • Gaps matter as much as findings. What a dossier does not support is often the most important result of a review.
  • People make the decision. Deep Proof prepares the review. Your team reads the reasoning and makes the call.
  • The platform should be open. Organisations handling confidential files should be able to inspect and run the software themselves.
