About Deep Proof

Make the reasoning part of the decision.

Deep Proof is a document-review platform for teams making high-stakes, evidence-led decisions.

It combines an open-source foundation with tailored, validated Skills for specific review workflows. A Skill brings together instructions, analysis tools, verification cases and the calibration needed for a particular organisation and project type.

Deep Proof is part of Outfinity SARL, an Outfinity Venture Studio company based in Switzerland. We work with teams to prepare and validate the analysis capabilities that make a real review process dependable.

For a conversation about your use case, contact contact@deepseek.ch.
