Deep Proof, in practice

Complex documents need more than a prompt.

Create a project, add its documents, and review them against the criteria that matter. Deep Proof produces clear findings, a decision memo and the questions worth asking — with the evidence beside every claim.

A different kind of assistance

A chat is useful.

A review needs a method.

General-purpose chat01

Drop files in. Ask a question.

  • One-off answers vary with each prompt
  • Method and assumptions stay implicit
  • Hard to test, compare or govern
  • Sources may be cited, gaps may not be systematic

ChatGPT and Claude are powerful starting points. On their own, they do not create a review process tailored to your standards.

Deep Proof02

Define the method. Then apply it.

  • Validated Skills turn expertise into a repeatable workflow
  • Every criterion has clear instructions and verification
  • Evidence, gaps and uncertainty are explicit
  • Results stay reviewable by your team

Use your own AI subscription or a custom deployment designed around your privacy requirements.

The work behind reliable output

Skills are where the proof begins.

A Deep Proof Skill is not a prompt template. It is a prepared and validated analysis capability, calibrated for a particular company, project type and use case.

01

Review instructions

What to examine, how to reason and what a good finding looks like.

02

Analysis code & tools

Repeatable checks, calculations and source-handling logic where needed.

03

Verification suite

Test cases that challenge the Skill before it becomes part of a real decision workflow.

04

Calibration & approval

Refinement with your experts, thresholds and terminology, then ongoing stewardship.

Skill file / instructions
# Revenue quality review Assess concentration across customers and channels. Flag any dependency above the approved threshold. Cite every conclusion. # Report uncertainty When sources disagree, show the conflict.
