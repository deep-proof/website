Deep Proof, in practice

Complex documents need more than a prompt.

Create a project, add its documents, and review them against the criteria that matter. Deep Proof produces clear findings, a decision memo and the questions worth asking, with the evidence beside every claim.

A different kind of assistance

A chat is useful.

A proper review needs a method.

General-purpose chat01

Drop files in. Ask a question.

  • One-off answers vary with each prompt
  • Method and assumptions stay implicit
  • Hard to test, compare or govern
  • Sources may be cited, but gaps are not checked systematically

ChatGPT and Claude are powerful starting points. On their own, they do not create a review process tailored to your standards.

Deep Proof02

Define the method. Then apply it.

  • Validated AI Review Skills turn expertise into a repeatable workflow
  • Every criterion has clear instructions and verification
  • Evidence, gaps and uncertainty are explicit
  • Results stay reviewable by your team

Use your own AI subscription or a custom deployment designed around your privacy requirements.

Questions we hear first

Before you book a call.

How is this different from uploading files to ChatGPT or Claude?

A chat answers the question you typed. Deep Proof applies a defined review method: the same criteria, instructions and checks every time, with evidence, gaps and uncertainty recorded for each criterion. The method can be tested, versioned and governed like any other part of your process.

Which AI models does Deep Proof use?

You can connect your own AI subscription, under the terms you already have with that provider, or use a custom deployment designed around your privacy requirements. The choice is made per organisation during scoping.

Where do our documents go?

That depends on the deployment you choose. When you run the platform in your own infrastructure, documents are stored there and text is only sent to the AI provider you configure. The security overview explains the options.

Does Deep Proof make the decision?

No. Deep Proof makes its reasoning inspectable: findings, citations, contradictions and open questions. Your team reads the reasoning and makes the call.

What is free and what is paid?

The Community Edition platform is open source under GPL-3.0. The commercial work is preparing, validating and calibrating AI Review Skills for your use case, and supporting the implementation. See licensing for details.
