Privacy

Your information deserves a clear frame.

This page describes how the Deep Proof website handles contact information.

Website contact

If you use the “Discuss your use case” form, the website opens your own email application with a draft addressed to contact@deepseek.ch. The form does not submit its contents to a website server.

How we use a message

Information you choose to send by email is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss a possible Deep Proof project and arrange a conversation. We do not sell personal information.

Controller and contact

Deep Proof is part of Outfinity SARL. For privacy questions or requests concerning your message, contact contact@deepseek.ch.
