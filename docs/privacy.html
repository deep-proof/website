Privacy

Your information deserves a clear frame.

This notice explains how Outfinity SARL handles personal data in connection with this website and enquiries about Deep Proof. It is written for the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Controller

Outfinity SARL, trading as Outfinity Venture Studio, Switzerland, is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: . Full details are in the Impressum.

Visiting this website

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It loads no third-party fonts, scripts or embedded content.
  • Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider that processes technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and time of access, in server logs. This is needed to deliver the pages securely.

The contact form

The “Discuss your use case” form does not send anything to a website server. When you submit it, your own email application opens with a draft addressed to . Nothing is transmitted until you send that email yourself.

When you email us

  • What we receive: your name, email address, company and whatever you write in the message.
  • Why: to answer your enquiry, discuss a possible Deep Proof project and arrange a conversation.
  • How long: for as long as needed for that purpose and any resulting business relationship, then deleted unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Who else sees it: our email provider, as a processor. We may share it with Axiologic Research where needed to answer a technical question about your project. We do not sell personal data.

Please do not send confidential client documents by email before we have agreed how they will be handled.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing. Write to . You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

The Deep Proof platform

This notice covers the website only. How documents are handled inside a Deep Proof deployment depends on where it runs and which AI provider you connect. See the security overview.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change.
