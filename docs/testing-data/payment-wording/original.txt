Grant committee minute book

Procedure. Net payments are payments made less money returned. A treasurer's retrospective summary repeats earlier entries and changes no balance.

Meeting of 12 April
The committee paid 470 francs to Willow Glass and 320 francs to Maple Ceramics.

Meeting of 10 May

Willow Glass sent a photograph of its completed window.

The project mentioned in the preceding paragraph returned 70 francs from its April payment.

The committee paid 150 francs to Maple Ceramics.

Meeting of 14 June
For the record, the treasurer summarised the April payments: 470 francs to Willow Glass and 320 francs to Maple Ceramics. This closed the retrospective summary.
