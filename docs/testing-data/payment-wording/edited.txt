Grant committee minute book

Procedure. Net payments are payments made less money returned. A treasurer's retrospective summary repeats earlier entries and changes no balance.

Meeting of 12 April
The committee paid 470 francs to Willow Glass and 320 francs to Maple Ceramics.

Meeting of 10 May

Willow Glass sent a photograph of its completed window.

The undertaking just described sent 70 francs of its April cheque back to the fund.

The committee paid 150 francs to Maple Ceramics.

Meeting of 14 June
For the record, the treasurer summarised the April payments. A cheque for 470 francs was drawn in favour of Willow Glass. A cheque for 320 francs was drawn in favour of Maple Ceramics. This closed the retrospective summary.
