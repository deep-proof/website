Committee review exercise

Judge each report claim against the source minutes. Mark each claim supported
or refuted and give the integer amount the sources establish in either case.
Amounts are in credits. “Held” is the amount transferred to a project less
what the committee has received back from it. Start all balances at zero.
Output only a JSON object mapping claim IDs to [verdict, integer].

at the first meeting. The treasurer transferred 9 credits to Elm Workshop.
The secretary confirmed the venue for the next meeting.

Minutes, third meeting
Elm Workshop sent back 4 credits. The decision on the next award was deferred.
